Possibly the worst I ever ever felt on a run, and I am not sure why. 

The run itself was "not very far" - only 5k over 35 mins. Come to think of it, it is pretty crazy that that is no longer a long run for me. Anyway, for the entire run my body was just feeling sluggish and sore. I felt like I had zero energy, despite cracking open a SAP pouch. I just took it nice and easy, and I got it done. I think sometimes you just can't escape the reality that running can be hard, and that's just that.

I think perhaps I haven't be sleeping enough, or doing enough stretching, and so on this run I made a commitment to myself that I would try to be better at those things. Let's see.
