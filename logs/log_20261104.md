Here is recipe for the perfect evening run.

Take 1 cup of uneven pavement, add 1/2 cup of tree roots, and mix until the surface starts to crack open. Remove all light, so you can't see that happening. Dust with slippery leaves.

In a separate bowl, take approx. 100 people with no awareness of others and ask them to find the most insane way to use a pavement.

Combine in a baking try and place in the oven at 2 million degrees for 25 mins, or until your house burns down.

Serve tepid, and enjoy!
