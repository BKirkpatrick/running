A refreshing wee run where I got absolutely smashed by the rain, and the new shoes got their first taste of _the elements_.

The rain was so heavy, and it came on so suddenly, that honestly it was pretty hilarious. Luckily I was running around the #meadows so there were a few stretches of the run that were under tree cover.

Body felt pretty good, though I did discover one downside to running on top of two-inches-worth of cushiony softness - if you stray from you Elven-path you are going to roll your ankle.
