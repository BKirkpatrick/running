A nice little recovery run, but the jumper was a mistake.

Not much to say about this one. A bit later in the day than I normally run, but the good thing about running is that it is, in principle, easy to fit around "real life". I must have run for 20 mins, at a decent recovery pace, and I could feel some fatigue in my calves from long run the day before. 

I also overestimated how cold it was. Despite it being night time it was surprisingly mild, and I had committed to running in a jumper. After about 3 minutes I was regretting that decision. I think some romantic imagery of Harvard students jogging through autumn trees must have wormed into my subconscious at some point. How foolish.

Note to future self: I'm built for the cold, not the heat.
