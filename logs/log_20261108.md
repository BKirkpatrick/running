I couldn't be bothered with this run. Knowing that I had to run 18 km was a little daunting, but I was able to use a little trick I have learned through my running journey so far: if you can get to the starting line everything else takes care of itself.

It also helped that I had promised myself a donut upon completion of the run.

I basically smashed the run. I had a nice warm up over the first few kilometres, and was able to find a really nice groove. Whenever my focus drifted too far I was able to pull my attention back to the run, and as a result my pace steadily improved the farther I went. Again I ended up improving on the pace I set in my previous long run. This is a good thing.

I thought a lot about how distances that were once big mental barriers are now just milestones that I cruise past. There is no logical reason that that should not continue to be true. So, being only 3 km away from a half marathon, with my pace already at a great place (and improving...), I am feeling pretty confident that I will be able to put in a good showing in the marathon come May.

I just have to keep showing up to those starting lines.
