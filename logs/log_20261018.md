A new longest run - 8 miles. Knees weak, arms spaghetti. There's spaghetti on his spaghetti already, Mom's spaghetti. And so on.

Snapping back to reality, this was another goooood run. It really gives me confidence that I pretty likely to finish the marathon if I stick to my training. That is a really nice feeling.

I spent most of the run thinking *around* running. I was thinking how I might visualise my running data, what I might write about in this log, who in their right mind might read it, and so on. One thing I realised is that part of the appeal of running, for me, is that it is a slow and analogue activity. You can't visualise or write about much unless you are slowly accumulating a lot of data - and that gives you a lot of time to think about it!
