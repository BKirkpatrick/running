Today I am changed.

Before today I had never run a half marathon. Nor had I ever observed an ice-skating moorhen. I don't yet know how these changes will shape my future - I just know there is no going back now.

My time for the half was 2:11:39, which I am kinda amazed by. My focus remains the marathon, but getting so close to the 2 hour mark on my first attempt at the half marathon gives me a real sense of joy and accomplishment.

And the crazy thing is that that time could easily have been faster. With 5 km left to go my body started to give up. My legs started to seize up and my hip flexors started to hurt. If I can figure out a way through this barrier, and I can put in a solid performance over the whole run duration, then who knows how my next half marathon will go!
