I just ran 12 km. That is the the furthest distance that I have ever ran in my entire life.

It is a funny feeling. Despite the fact that I have committed to run a marathon, I hadn't really accepted that I would be setting any athletics "firsts". I think I have been holding onto an unspoken assumption that I was past my prime (perhaps I am), and therefore I couldn't surpass any of the outstanding achievemnts of my early twenties. But now that is evidently untrue...

I didn't even find the run today all that challenging - a blessing given how I have been feeling recently - which gives me real pause for thought. What are we really capable of? Do I typically give myself permission to "think big" about what is possible for myself? I am not sure I do... but perhaps I could be better at that - and perhaps running can help me do that!
