A short run today - scheduled for 15 mins but I ended up running for 17 or 18 instead. I tend to run in an `out and back' style, and I have developed the habit of running a bit past half way before I turn around. I really like doing this for a few reasons:

1. It ensures that I never reach my house with some running still to do.
2. It gives me the option to finish a run with a few minutes of a cool-down walk.
3. By the time I have finished the prescribed run I often feel pretty good and I want to play around with my running for a couple of minutes. Sometimes this means running fast, or being extra bouncy in my strides - really it is just whatever mood takes me in the moment.

I found the run very easy today, though I noticed some fatigue in my hamstrings. Recovery runs are a great way to get some feedback on how my muscles are feeling.

Oh, also, it was great to not be cold and soaking wet. Would recommend.
