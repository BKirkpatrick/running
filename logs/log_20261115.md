Alright so first of all, Nike tried to do me dirty on this one. This was advertised as a 5km run, but when I went to select my audio guide, my app was all like "oh hey, we recommend this run that lasts 99 minutes...". No chance mate - I have been preparing for this 5 km race all week.

Off on my race I went, and I fear I came out of the gates way too hot. Usually I am pretty disciplined about starting slowly and increasing my pace as the run progresses, but today I thought "this is a race. Start at 5 km race pace". Apparently that was not the correct strategy, because after 2 km I was feeling pretty gassed. 

I am taking it as a valuable learning experience, because now I know what it feels like to push too hard too fast. I was targetting a time of 25 mins, which I didn't quite achieve. I'm interested to see what I can achieve on my next 5 km race, and I will be sure to start a bit more easily!
